Account deletion

Last updated: May 2026

How to request deletion

Email info@centifi.app from the email address associated with your Centifi account.

In your email, include:

  • The account email address you use in Centifi
  • Your request: “Delete my account”

What gets deleted

  • Your Centifi account profile
  • Your synced expenses, lists, categories, and preferences stored on our servers

Retention

We aim to process deletion requests within a reasonable time. If we must retain certain records for legal or security reasons, we will limit retention to what is required.
